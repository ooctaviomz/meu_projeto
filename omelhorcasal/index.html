About me

Born and based in a small town in Brazil, I'm a technology passionate who enjoys learning things constantly. I've been working in the IT field since 2006, most of this time with Virtualization, Windows and Linux servers, installing and maintaining all kinds of network services.

Currently a graduate student (MBA - Business Management) at FGV - Fundação Getúlio Vargas, I also have an associate degree in Information Technology Management by Infnet Institute (RJ - Brazil).

I possess multiple certifications from Microsoft, LPI (Linux Professional Institute) and Cisco.

Development competencies: HTML5, JavaScript, CSS3, Bootstrap, Git, Ruby on Rails, C#.
Infrasctructure competencies: Microsoft OSes (Windows XP, 7 ,2003, 2008, 2012), Linux, Networking (Cisco and SMB devices), VMWare (ESXi, vCenter 5.5, SRM, Update Manager).
